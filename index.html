A softer place
to land.

Do your thoughts race long after the day is done?

Are you running on empty, pushing through stress and burnout while feeling disconnected from yourself? Maybe a major life transition has left you unsure of who you are or where you’re headed. Anxiety, depression, low self-esteem, and relationship challenges can make everyday life feel heavy, but you don’t have to carry it alone.

I support adults at every stage, from college students and young adults to busy professionals and older adults. With compassionate support, you can feel calmer, rebuild your confidence, and rediscover hope.

I can work with you as you navigate anxiety, stress, burnout, and depression, along with self-esteem, relationship challenges, and life transitions. Whether you’re a college student finding your footing, a professional stretched thin, or an older adult facing a new chapter, therapy can help you quiet worry, lift your mood, set healthy boundaries, and feel more confident in who you are and where you’re headed.

You can experience a warm, judgment-free space where you can slow down and feel heard. With CBT, you can notice and shift the thoughts that fuel anxiety and low mood. DBT skills can help calm overwhelming emotions and stress. My trauma-informed, strengths-based approach can help you gain clarity, build coping skills, and move forward with confidence.

Reaching out for support can feel like a big step, especially when you’re already exhausted. You don’t need to have it all figured out first. Change often begins with small, steady steps, and over time you can start to feel more grounded, more confident, and more like yourself again.

A little about
who you'll meet.

I believe therapy works best when you feel comfortable being yourself. My hope is to offer a welcoming place where we can look at what's difficult, make room for what matters, and find a way forward together.

Warm Willow Resilience is a practice shaped by warmth, curiosity, and the belief that growth can happen at your own pace.

When I’m not working, you’ll often find me staying active through marathon running, swimming, and cycling. Being active is something I truly enjoy, and I believe that taking care of our physical health can have such a positive impact on our mental and emotional well-being. I especially enjoy encouraging and supporting others in finding ways to care for themselves and feel strong, confident, and connected.

I also love spending time with animals and being involved in my community. Volunteering and connecting with others through community activities are important to me because I value creating meaningful relationships and making a positive difference in the lives of those around me. These experiences are a big part of who I am and continue to shape the way I connect with others in my work as a therapist.

I believe therapy is about more than working through challenges—it’s about feeling supported, finding connection, discovering your strengths, and creating a life that feels meaningful to you.
